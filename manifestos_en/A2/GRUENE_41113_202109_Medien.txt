Public service broadcasting for all and a diverse media landscape
Critical and independent journalism is a pillar of our democracy.
We stand for a pluralistic, critical and non-state public service broadcasting for all, as well as for the quality and diversity of the private and non-profit media landscape.
To ensure that public service broadcasting is strong and future-proof, we work for functional financing that follows a defined programme mandate.
Because it is financed by everyone, it must reach everyone.
Due to its special position and the claim to represent the diversity of the living environments, opinions and interests of the population, its need for reform also arises.
The digitisation of public service broadcasting must be pushed forward and its current offerings must be reviewed.
To this end, we want to launch an initiative together with the countries and initiate a social debate.
We are committed to broadcasting councils that better reflect the diversity of today's society, become more assertive, and become more remote from broadcasters and the state.
The media libraries of the public authorities should be permanently accessible and interlinked at European level, provided that the authors are adequately remunerated.
Local media need support that is coordinated with the countries and organised away from the state.
Quality journalism needs considerably better framework conditions, for example through improvements in source protection and information rights or the opening up of the artists "social insurance fund for journalists, including the obligation to contribute to media platforms.
Non-profit journalism needs legal certainty.
